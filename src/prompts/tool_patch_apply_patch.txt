Apply a standard GPT-style patch to existing UTF-8 files.

Use `*** Begin Patch`, one or more `*** Update File: path` sections with `@@`
hunks, and `*** End Patch`. Hunk lines begin with a space for context, `-` for
removed content, or `+` for added content. Use `write`, `delete`, and `rename`
instead of `*** Add File`, `*** Delete File`, or move operations.
