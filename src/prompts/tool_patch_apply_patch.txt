Apply a standard GPT-style patch to UTF-8 files.

Use `*** Begin Patch` and `*** End Patch` around one or more operations. Use
`*** Add File: path` followed by `+` content lines to create a file, `*** Delete
File: path` to delete one, or `*** Update File: path` with `@@` hunks to edit
one. An update may include `*** Move to: new-path` before its hunks to rename
or move the edited file. Hunk lines begin with a space for context, `-` for
removed content, or `+` for added content.
